Protothon 2025

A redesign of Devaki Dhuldhoya's Photography for Protothon 2025.

I collaborated with a team to deliver a redesign of Devaki Dhuldhoya Photography for Protothon 2025.

The classic track required my team to deliver a redesign of Devaki Dhuldhoya's website so it could attract new leads and allow customers to buy prints directly from her site.

To brainstorm an effective redesign, I, along with my teammates, completed a comparison sheet between Devaki's webpage and her competitors. We concluded that the redesign needed to have a visually appealing appearence in an understated way that makes her photos stand out, including a simplified navigation bar and an added shop page.
